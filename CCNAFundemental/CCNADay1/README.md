**5)**What Is Different Connecting Network Device?
--------------------------------------------------------------------
--There Are Different Computer Network Devices Like:
-----------------------------------------------------
*5.1)**What Is The Switch?
--------------------------------
5.1.1)**It Has A Lot Of Network Interfaces , Or Network Ports To Plug Servers , And Clients Within The Same Switch...

------------------------------------------------------------
5.1.2)**It Provides Connectivity Between Different Clients Within The Same LAN (Local Area Network)... Like Small Office , Home , And More...

--------------------------------------------------------
5.1.3)**It Does Not Provide Connectivity Between Different LANS (Local Area Networks) Over The Internet...

--------------------------------------------------------
5.1.4)**It Is Mainly Used To Forward Or Send DATA (Traffic) For All Different Clients Within The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...

--------------------------------------------------------
5.1.5)**It Can Not Connect To The Internet , And Sending DATA (Traffic) Between Different Clients Between Different LANS (Local Area Networks) Over The Internet...

---------------------------------------------------------
![NetworkSwitch](https://github.com/mahmoudkhalidshabaan300/CCNAJermeyITFullCourse/blob/main/CCNAFundemental/CCNADay1/Day1Images/TwoSwitches.png)

----------------------------------------------------------
*5.2)**What Is The Router?
------------------------------------------
5.2.1)**Router Has A Fewer Network Interfaces , Or Fewer Network Ports Than The Switch...

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2.2)**Router Provides Connectivity Between Different LANS (Local Area Networks) Over The Internet...

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2.3)**Router Does Not Provide Connectivity Between Different Clients Within The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2.4)**Router Is Mainly Used To Connect Between Different Clients Within The Same LAN (Local Area Network)...

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2.5)**Router Is Mainly Therefore Used To Send DATA (Traffic) Over The Internet...

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

![NetworkRouter](https://github.com/mahmoudkhalidshabaan300/CCNAJermeyITFullCourse/blob/main/CCNAFundemental/CCNADay1/Day1Images/TwoRouters.png)

*5.4)**What Is The Firewall?
---------------------------------------
5.4.1)**Firewall Is A Specialty Network Security Device That Control , And Monitor DATA (Traffic) That Entering , Or Exiting Your Own Network...

----------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4.2)**Firewall Must Be Configured With Security Rules To Determine Which Traffic Should Be Allowed , Or Denied...

---------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4.3)**Firewall Controls , And Monitors Traffic (DATA) Based On Configured Rules...

---------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4.4)**Firewall Permits , Or Denies Passing Traffic (DATA) According To Different Security Policies...

---------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4.5)**Firewall Known As A Next Generation Firewall When They Include More Modern , And Advanced Filtering Capabilities...

------------------------
*1)**Network Firewall:
------------------------
--Network Firewall Is A Hardware Device , It Is Mainly Used To Filtering Traffic (DATA) Between Different Networks...

*2)**Host-Based Firewall:
---------------------------
--Host-Based Firewall Is A Software Device , It Is Mainly To Filter The Traffic (DATA) That Entering , Or Exiting The Host Machine Like Personal Computer (PC) , Smart Phone , And More...
